ecns graded — name cards

every ecns name is certified with an eg (ecns grade) from 1.0 to 10.0.
four sub-grades — scarcity, composition, resonance, structure — sum to a raw score mapped to half-point increments.
the grade badge appears on every card. provenance tracks acquisition method: direct registration or mystery pull.

grade distribution

most names land 3.0–6.0. dictionary words push into 7.0+. short + euphonious + pattern = 9.0+.
a 10.0 requires the trifecta: short + euphonious + palindrome + dictionary word.

mystery box — pull random names

pay a steep discount, get a random name, discover its grade at reveal.
most pulls are common (eg 3–5), but the chance of pulling a gem (eg 8+) creates excitement and repeat purchases.
provenance: "mystery pull" — like "1st edition" on trading cards.
